How to Install on Mobile

For Android (Chrome):

  1. Open your website in Chrome
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) in the top right
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
  4. Confirm the installation

For iOS (Safari):

  1. Open your website in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (□ with arrow)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Name the app and tap "Add"

🔗 How to Share Instagram Reels to Your App

Using Instagram:

  1. Open Instagram and find a Reel you want to download
  2. Tap the Share button (paper plane icon)
  3. Look for "Reel Downloader" in the share sheet
  4. Tap it to open the app with the link pre-filled
  5. A beautiful overlay will appear showing the shared link
  6. Tap "Download This Reel" to process it

From Browser:

  1. Copy an Instagram Reel link
  2. Open your browser's share menu
  3. Select "Reel Downloader"
  4. The app will open with the link ready to download

Testing the PWA

Important: To test the PWA features:
  • Deploy your app to a server with HTTPS (required for PWA)
  • Or use ngrok or similar tools to create an HTTPS tunnel for testing
  • The app won't work as PWA on http://localhost on mobile devices

Files Created

  • static/manifest.json - PWA manifest with app info and share target
  • static/sw.js - Service worker for offline support
  • Updated templates/base.html - Added PWA meta tags and share overlay
  • Updated templates/index.html - Added share handling JavaScript
  • Updated app.py - Added /share-target endpoint

Customization

You can customize the PWA by editing static/manifest.json:
