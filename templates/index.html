Frequently asked questions

Common answers to help you troubleshoot faster.

Can I download private reels?

No - only publicly accessible reels can be fetched. If you own the account, switch it to public or download from within the Instagram app.

The link I pasted says it is invalid. Why?

Double-check the URL starts with https://www.instagram.com/ and contains /reel/, /p/, or /tv/. Try tapping Copy Link in the Instagram app and paste again.

Where do my downloads go?

Downloads live in a secure temporary folder on the server. They are auto-deleted after a few hours or as soon as you leave the results page.
